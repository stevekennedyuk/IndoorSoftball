Privacy Policy

Indoor Softball Scorer (iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch) · Last updated 8 November 2026

Summary

Indoor Softball Scorer does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not connect to any NetTek server.

What the app stores

Team names, game settings, scores and the history of finished games are stored only on your device, in the app's own private storage. You can delete them at any time from the History screen, or by deleting the app.

Saving and sharing results

When a game finishes, the app saves its results (team names, location, start time and scores) as a spreadsheet file on your device. You can see the file in the Files app under On My iPhone/iPad → Indoor Softball, and you can delete it at any time. Results only leave your device if you choose to send them with "Share results", and they go only to the people or apps you pick. NetTek never receives them.

iPhone and Apple Watch sync

If you use the app on a paired iPhone and Apple Watch, settings and finished games are copied directly between your own devices using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. NetTek never receives this information.

Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device to warn you when a half inning is nearly over or has ended. They are created on the device and are not sent through any server.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Data controller and contact

NetTek Ltd, London, United Kingdom. For questions about this policy, email support@nettek.uk. Under UK GDPR you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
